Why I Chose UMD

The City of Duluth

Having been through Duluth many times as a kid, the beauty and location of the city was a huge factor in choosing to go here. It's just so fun!

Outdoor Recreation

There are so many Outdoor Recreation groups on campus to get involved with. From Nordic Skiing to Whitewater Kayaking, there'll always be something to do outside!

The Community

Minnesotans are such friendly people, and those that are here are no exception. People are super open to being friends and going out to do things!

Transportation

Students can use their ID card as a free bus pass, allowing easy access to the rest of the city for jobs or recreation. Many of the bus lines start or go right through campus, so it's nice and convenient.

What I Love About Duluth

Duluth is the perfect mix of so many things that make cities great, all in one on the North Shore. It has plenty of outdoor amenities, such as 7+ Nordic Skiing trail networks, disc golf courses, the Superior Hiking Trail, and who could forget the lake! Moving just outside the city, the 7 State Parks of the North Shore are all within a 3 hours drive, and Canada is just a little further to explore natural areas and Crown Land near Thunder Bay and other parts of Northern Ontario. In contrast, Duluth has many places to go out for nightlife, places to shop, and a D1 hockey team to cheer for. No matter what you're into, Duluth will have something for you.

Campus & City Highlights

Canal Park

The most touristy part of town, with shops and good views of Lake Superior. A good spot to go for a run, skip rocks, or just sit and work on something.

AMSOIL Arena

The arena where both the UMD Mens and Womens D1 hockey teams play. Always loud with support from the city, Go Bulldogs!

Park Point

The longest freshwater sand bar in the world, the lake side is a long beach to go to in the summer. There's also a hiking trail that lets you walk all the way to the end, with old buildings and good views on the way.

Bagley Nature Area

My UMD Experience

Everything I've experienced at UMD has confirmed that I made the right choice in coming here for my degree. As soon as I got here it was so easy to make friends and find a community of people I could be around. One of my best memories so far was taking a trip to Marquette, Michigan with the Nordic Ski Club for the Noquemanon Ski Marathon. I had so much fun with everyone on the team, and led me to one of the best ski trails I've ever been on!
